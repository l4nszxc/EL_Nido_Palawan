Tourism Products & Attractions

Iconic Wonders of Bacuit Bay & Inland Palawan

Featured directly from our official municipal travel itinerary. Explore emerald lagoons, limestone bastions, vibrant coral sanctuaries, and endless golden shores.

Tour A Highlight

Big Lagoon

Min landscape

Brochure Detail: Kayaking through crystal turquoise water surrounded by towering limestone cliffs. The crown jewel of Miniloc Island where motorized boats remain moored while visitors navigate tranquil shallows in tandem kayaks.

Duration: 1.5 - 2 Hours
Kayak Rental: ₱300 (2-Person Tandem)
Availability: Daily with regulated slot permit
Book Tour with Big Lagoon
Secret Pool

Secret Lagoon

Natural Wonder

Brochure Detail: Discover the hidden lagoon and enjoy swimming in its enclosed natural setting. Accessed through a small keyhole opening in the sheer karst wall, leading to a secluded natural pool surrounded by soaring rock domes.

Duration: 45 Minutes - 1 Hour
Inclusion: Included in ₱8,500 Package & Tour A
Access: Low/Mid tide swim & crawl portal
Reserve Secret Lagoon Visit
Reef Sanctuary

Shimizu Island

Snorkel & Lunch

Brochure Detail: Snorkeling and marine-life viewing with an iconic beachfront island seafood feast. Famed for its deep coral shelves where green sea turtles and vibrant clownfish colonies swim in high visibility.

Duration: 1.5 Hours (Snorkel + Lunch)
Equipment: Free Snorkel Mask & Life Vest
Feast: Grilled fish, squid, pork, & tropical fruits
Book Shimizu Island Tour
Tropical Chill

Seven Commandos Beach

White Sand Beach

Brochure Detail: Swimming, relaxing, and enjoying tropical beach scenery under lush coconut groves. Lined with soft powder-white sand, beach volleyball nets, and rustic coconut smoothie shacks.

Duration: 1 Hour relaxation
Inclusions: Boat stop, beach access, swimming
Atmosphere: Family friendly & sunset chill
Inquire for Commandos Beach
Twin Beaches

Nacpan & Calitang Beach

Inland Landmark

Brochure Detail: Long stretch of white sand ideal for swimming and photography. Enjoy the scenery of the famous Nacpan-Calitang twin beaches, where two curving shorelines converge at a lush green promontory.

Distance: 45 Mins North of El Nido Poblacion
Transport: Included Land Transfer in 4D3N Package
Length: 4 km of pristine golden sands
Book Twin Beach Tour
Sunset Haven

Las Cabañas Beach

Sunset Capital

Brochure Detail: Sunset viewing and coastal relaxation. Facing west towards Bacuit Bay, this lively stretch features beach clubs, ambient acoustic lounges, and an inter-island zipline directly over the bay.

Best Time: 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM
Adventure: 750m Inter-island Zipline
Vibe: Seaside dining and acoustic music
Reserve Sunset Excursion
