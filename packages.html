4 Days & 3 Nights Island Escape

Our flagship municipal holiday package combining the world-famous Tour A Island-Hopping with Northern Palawan’s most celebrated inland coastline. Designed for first-time visitors and couples seeking complete convenience.

Suggested Day-by-Day Itinerary:
Day 1: Arrival & Town Welcome

Arrival at El Nido/PPS, check-in to accommodation, briefing on eco-rules, explore El Nido Town proper.

Day 2: Tour A Island Hopping

Big Lagoon kayaking, Secret Lagoon swim, Shimizu Island reef snorkeling & grilled seafood feast, Seven Commandos beach chill.

Day 3: Inland Beach Safari & Sunset

Scenic countryside van drive to Nacpan-Calitang Twin Beaches. Afternoon coastal drinks and sunset at Las Cabañas Beach.

Day 4: Breakfast & Departure

Leisurely Filipino breakfast, souvenir shopping at municipal artisanal stalls, land transfer to airport or PPS van terminal.
