Helpful Travel Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding municipal regulations, eco-tourism fees, and lagoon permits.

Yes. Under Municipal Ordinance, all tourists entering the El Nido Protected Area must pay a ₱200 Eco-Tourism Development Fee (ETDF). This ticket is valid for 10 consecutive days and directly finances coral reef conservation, environmental patrols, and municipal waste management.

Strictly No. El Nido strictly prohibits single-use plastic water bottles, disposable bags, and plastic food wrappers on all boat tours. Travelers are advised to bring refillable water tumblers. Potable water is provided onboard all municipal certified boats.

To protect marine biodiversity, the local government enforces carrying capacity slots. Guests are required to obtain entry permits (which our accredited packages arrange in advance). Inside the Big Lagoon, motorized boats remain anchored at the entrance while guests kayak inside.

You can fly directly to El Nido Lio Airport (ENI) via AirSWIFT, or fly to Puerto Princesa International Airport (PPS) and take an air-conditioned shared or private van (approx. 5 to 6 hours scenic highway drive). Detailed instructions are provided in our Travel Guide.
