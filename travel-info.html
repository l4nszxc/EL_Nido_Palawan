Logistics & Access

How to Get to El Nido, Palawan

Located approximately 238 kilometers north of Palawan’s capital (Puerto Princesa), travelers can choose between direct flights or scenic overland transfers.

Carrier: AirSWIFT operates direct commercial turbo-prop flights connecting Manila (NAIA T4), Cebu (MCIA), Clark, and Caticlan directly to Lio Airport.
Travel Time: Approx. 1 hour and 15 minutes.
Airport Transfer: Lio Airport is just 20 minutes by tricycle or resort van to El Nido Poblacion.

Budget-Friendly: Fly major airlines (Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, AirAsia) to Puerto Princesa International Airport.
Overland Van: Board an air-conditioned shared or chartered tourist passenger van directly at PPS airport to El Nido.
Travel Time: 5 to 6 hours across the scenic Palawan North Highway with halfway dining stopover in Roxas.
Cost: Approx. ₱600 - ₱700 per person one-way.

High-speed passenger ferries (Montenegro Shipping, Atienza, Jomalia) connect Coron Town to El Nido Port daily.
Travel Time: Approx. 3.5 to 4 hours across the Linapacan Strait.

Climate & Planning

When to Visit El Nido

Dry Season (Peak Season)
November to May

The premier window for calm seas, minimal rainfall, and turquoise lagoon clarity. Ideal for snorkeling in Shimizu Island and kayaking in Big Lagoon. Book tours 2-4 weeks in advance.

Avg Temp: 27°C - 33°C • Clear skies
Shoulder Months
June & October

Fewer tourists, lower accommodation tariffs, and occasional refreshing rain showers. Sea conditions are generally favorable with pleasant island tours.

Avg Temp: 26°C - 30°C • Great value
Habagat / Wet Season
July to September

Southwest monsoon rains. Philippine Coast Guard may issue small craft gale warnings halting boat tours if swells occur. Inland safaris to Nacpan Beach remain accessible.

Always check Coast Guard advisory
Tourist Preparation

What to Pack & Dress Code

Prepare for an active eco-adventure on outrigger boats, shallow coral reefs, and sun-soaked sandbars.

No Single-Use Plastics: Disposable water bottles will be confiscated at the boarding beach by municipal eco-wardens.
Essential Gear
  • • 10L - 20L Waterproof Dry Bag
  • • Refillable Water Tumbler
  • • Neoprene Aqua Shoes (for limestone)
  • • Snorkel mask (or use included gear)
Sun & Skin Protection
  • • Reef-Safe Biodegradable Sunscreen
  • • UV Rashguard or Swim Shirt
  • • Polarized Sunglasses & Sun Hat
  • • Quick-dry micro-fiber towel
Money & Connectivity
  • • Sufficient Philippine Peso Cash (₱)
  • • Power Bank for phones/cameras
  • • Waterproof phone pouch with lanyard
  • • Local SIM / eSIM (Globe / Smart)
Personal Health
  • • Personal maintenance medication
  • • Motion sickness pills (for boat swells)
  • • Insect repellent lotion
  • • Rehydration salts
Environmental Stewardship

El Nido Eco-Code of Conduct

Bacuit Bay is a critically protected Biosphere Reserve. Every tourist is legally expected to uphold the Leave No Trace policy under Municipal Environmental Ordinances.

Do Not Step on Live Corals: Float gently or paddle. Coral formations take centuries to grow.
No Wildlife Harassment: Do not feed monkeys, touch sea turtles, or chase marine life.
Take Nothing but Memories: Collecting shells, coral sand, or limestone rock is strictly illegal.
